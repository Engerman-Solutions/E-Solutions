Nonprofits & associations · onboarding

One-time setup questionnaire — ten questions, answered once.

About ten minutes. Some answers take a minute; a few need looking up. Answer what you can and leave the rest — we fill it from the ledger and your Form 990, and your onboarding walk-through covers anything unclear. No files are uploaded here; the ledger, budget and contracts go to your secure workspace folder.

Received — thank you. Your answers are with Matthew. The next things you will see are the completeness note within one business day and your onboarding walk-through invitation.
Who is answering
1 · Legal name and tax status Why we ask: functional columns, donor restrictions, Schedules A and B, lobbying notices.
2 · Fiscal year end Sets the months, the prior-year comparison and the filing calendar.
3 · Board and finance committee dates for the year We work backward from each meeting to your intake and delivery dates.
4 · Who approves the packet, and who receives it? Who approves entries, who accepts the memo, who receives cash alerts.
5 · Cash or accrual? Date of the last bank reconciliation? We rely on your reconciled books and say so; a cash-basis memo is labeled as such.
6 · Donor-restricted grants or gifts Restricted money is reported separately and released as its purpose is met. Skip if your ledger already tracks restrictions by class.
7 · Reserve policy Sets the floor the memo measures cash against, and the reserve test.
8 · Program list Program results and full cost follow the way your board thinks about the organization.
9 · Monthly spread of the budget Only if your budget is annual. Confirms the seasonal plan the variance is measured against.
10 · Anything your board asks to see regularly Optional. We build it into the Board Brief so the packet answers it before it is asked.
Membership and learning systems Optional upgrade: unlocks membership and education KPIs and conference pacing.
