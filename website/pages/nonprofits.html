How it works

Four steps, on your board's calendar

1

Send three things

Ledger export, board-approved budget and event contracts; the setup questionnaire once, at onboarding.

2

Intake confirmed

Complete, or exactly what is missing, within one business day. The clock starts on complete intake.

3

Draft within 72 hours, then within 48

Every figure tied to your ledger by the workbook's checks; reviewed and signed by Matthew Engerman before your CEO sees it.

4

Review and circulate

Three business days for CEO review; circulated seven days before the meeting — so books close about two weeks ahead, or we present the prior month with a cash refresh.

What you receive

Three layers, one packet — routed by role

Directors get the Board Brief as its own PDF; the CEO, Treasurer and Finance Committee the full memo; events, membership, education and the bookkeeper the Framework tabs they use. A built-in update for each department — and no new job for the CEO.

Board Brief · three pages

What the board uses

Where the organization stands against plan and last year; the actions for the meeting, with motion wording drafted for the chair — the board adopts, amends or declines it; the quarter in three pictures. Every action labeled consent, note or vote; it says plainly when no funding decision is requested. Nothing is asked beyond page 2.

Management Summary

The evidence

One short section per subject: variance with evidence, forecast and cash, program financial sustainability, financial position, KPIs, risks, decisions, adjustments, and how the packet was checked. The CEO and Treasurer can answer any director's question from it; the auditor can trace every figure.

Nonprofit Budget Framework · Excel

The engine

Built at onboarding and kept current: budget by month, full-year forecast, cash floor, reserves, KPIs, program financial sustainability, event contracts and the downside scenarios — cancellation and attrition — Form 990 crosswalk, built-in checks. No separate fee.

Between packets: a monthly cash refresh with the Treasurer alert, so cash is watched every month whether or not the board meets.

What we do not ask for

ItemWhy not
Bank statementsWe rely on your reconciled books and say so in the memo; the questionnaire records the last reconciliation date.
Audited financial statementsNot needed to produce the memo; opening balances come from the ledger.
Form 990 — unless yours is on request onlyPosted publicly, we retrieve it ourselves at no cost to you. If yours is available on request only, send the most recent filing with the budget.

One service, three tax statuses

501(c)(3), (c)(6) or (c)(4) — the packet adapts

The memo and the workbook adapt to your tax status; the setup questionnaire tells us which applies. Tax items are screened, not decided — your tax adviser determines treatment and filings.

501(c)(3)

Donor-restricted gifts and grants reported separately and released as their purpose is met; functional reporting as your filings require.

501(c)(6)

Dues, events, sponsorship and education economics; the lobbying notice tracked where it applies.

501(c)(4)

Program and advocacy results kept distinct; the board sees the same three pages.
