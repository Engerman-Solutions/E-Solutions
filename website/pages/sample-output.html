Monthly variance memorandum

NovaCRM, Inc. — February 2026

Reviewed · Signed · Final

Executive summary

February revenue of $473.4K exceeded budget by $15.4K (+3.4%), driven by subscription growth that more than offset a shortfall in professional services. Total expenses exceeded budget by $14.1K (+3.3%), primarily legal costs tied to the Series B process.

Operating income of $31.3K beat plan by $1.3K (+4.3%). Gross margin held at 81.1% against 81.3% budgeted — stable unit economics despite the revenue mix shifting toward lower-margin professional services in prior periods.

Revenue variance

Subscriptions · +$17,800 (+4.5%) · favorable

Subscription revenue of $413.6K exceeded the $395.8K budget by $17.8K. Twelve mid-market logos closed in late January and activated in February, and gross churn came in at 0.8% against 1.2% budgeted. Net revenue retention of 112% continues to run ahead of the 108% plan assumption.

Professional services · −$6,500 (−14.4%) · watch

Professional services revenue of $38.7K fell short of the $45.2K budget. Two implementations (ClientCo and Meridian) slipped on client-side resourcing, moving $4.2K of recognized revenue into March; the remaining $2.3K is a scope reduction on the Apex onboarding engagement.

The cost-of-revenue overage of $4.2K is hosting cost for the new mid-market cohort. The operating-expense overage of $9.9K is $8.1K of unbudgeted Series B legal fees and $1.8K of recruiting cost for the senior engineer search. Excluding those one-time items, operating expense was on plan.

Recommended actions

  • Recover the services shortfall. Schedule Q2 implementation kickoffs for the deals now in negotiation so February's slip lands in March–April.
  • Complete the infrastructure cost review before the next billing cycle: reserved capacity and right-sizing, to hold cost of revenue.
  • Close the engineering hire to restore plan headcount and protect development velocity for the Q2 roadmap.
  • Cap Series B legal exposure. Confirm a fixed-fee arrangement with outside counsel for the remaining closing work.
