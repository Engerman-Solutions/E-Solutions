How we work

A person owns every packet. The tools do the arithmetic.

We use modern analysis tools, including AI, inside a process a person is accountable for. AI may draft the wording; every number comes from the workbook's calculations on your ledger; every draft is read line by line; and nothing reaches you until Matthew Engerman has reviewed and signed it. This page says plainly what that means for your numbers and your data.

The clock

From complete intake to a signed packet

1

Intake

Founders: ledger export and budget. Nonprofits: ledger export, board-approved budget and event contracts, plus the setup questionnaire once. We read your files; we never write back to your accounting system.

2

Completeness, in one business day

Complete, or exactly what is missing. The clock starts on complete intake — timestamped — and entries we propose during preparation are approved inside the clock; they do not restart it.

3

Build and check

Actuals posted to your framework; variance, forecast, cash and scenarios computed; every figure in the memo tied to the ledger by the workbook's own checks. If evidence is missing, the memo says so rather than guessing.

4

Review, sign, deliver

Read line by line, signed by the founder, delivered within 48 hours (within 72 for a first nonprofit packet) — the commitment is the ceiling, not the target. You review; your named approver signs off on any adjusting entries; then it goes to the board.

The checks

How a figure earns its place in the memo

  • It ties. Every number in the memo reconciles to the ledger through the framework; the workbook carries the checks, and the memo records how the packet was checked.
  • It is sourced. Where a figure came from — ledger, budget, contract, prior period — is recorded with the packet.
  • It is read. A person reads every draft for sense, not just for arithmetic: does this explanation hold up at the table?
  • It is signed. No packet is delivered without the founder's signature on it. If something is uncertain, the memo flags it; it does not round it off.

What that gets you

Five minutes at the table, and no surprises

The board reads three pages and knows where it stands and what it is being asked to do. The treasurer and the auditor can trace any figure back to its source. The executive who presents it is not defending numbers; they are explaining them, with the evidence already in hand.

Your data

Explicit commitments, in plain words

Only statements that are true for Engerman Solutions today.

Your files are used for your packet, and nothing else

Your data is never used to train any public or third-party AI model, and our AI provider is contractually barred from doing so. AI drafts wording only; every figure comes from the workbook's calculations on your ledger, so any number can be traced to its source. Nothing is sold, shared or repurposed.

Raw files are deleted within thirty days of delivery

Your ledger export, budget and contracts are deleted within thirty days of each packet's delivery — and files sent for a free sample, together with our copy of the sample, within thirty days as well. Your delivered packets stay in your workspace for as long as you are a client, with a full export on request if you leave.

Names are never required

Staffing is reported at the aggregate level by default; individual compensation only at the client's written election, with access restricted to the people you name. Payroll totals come from the ledger and headcounts from the optional membership report; names are never needed for the memo.

One client, one workspace

Each client's files live in a dedicated workspace inside our Microsoft 365 tenant in the United States — encrypted at rest and in transit, multi-factor authentication on every account, no anonymous sharing links, least-privilege access. Never copied to personal devices or third-party storage. We read your accounting data; we never write back to your system.

If something goes wrong, you hear within 24 hours

If we become aware of a security incident affecting your data, we notify you within 24 hours with what we know, what we are doing, and what, if anything, you need to do.

Paper before files

A mutual NDA is in place before any file is uploaded; a data processing agreement is available on request. Two sub-processors, named in your terms — Microsoft for hosting and identity, our AI provider for drafting — and you are told in advance if that ever changes. We do not claim certifications we do not hold.

The operative terms — service clock, data handling, confidentiality — are in the Service Terms and Engagement Schedule you sign; this page summarizes them. Questions: info@engermansolutions.com.

What we are not

Said once, clearly

  • Not bookkeeping. Your bookkeeper closes the books; we explain them. Monthly close and posting remain yours.
  • Not an audit, review or attestation. We rely on your reconciled books and say so in the memo. We do not certify the books; we explain them.
  • Not tax advice. Tax items are screened, not decided; your tax adviser determines treatment and filings.
  • Not a recommendation to adopt a motion. The Board Brief drafts the wording; the board adopts, amends or declines it.
