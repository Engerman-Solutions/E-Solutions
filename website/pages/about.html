About

Decisions, with precision.

Engerman Solutions exists for the owner, the founder and the executive director who answer to someone — a bank, an investor, a board, a funder — and who get numbers from their bookkeeper but not the explanation. We are the finance department those organizations would hire if they could: delivered, not staffed.

The founder

Matthew Engerman

Founder & CEO. A finance executive with more than fifteen years driving financial performance and operations, Matthew started Engerman Solutions on a plain observation: most people running a business cannot answer "do you know where you are?" with a number — not because they aren't smart, but because nobody ever built them a finance department. They have a bank balance, a bookkeeper they see quarterly, and a feeling.

The first answer was built by hand — eight years of monthly models for the firm's founding client, before Engerman Solutions had a name. Today it is the framework and the memo every client receives: a board-ready variance memo, a budget framework built and maintained for you, and a budget summary written in language an owner can use. Matthew reviews and signs every packet before it leaves the building.

Where it is going: a finance department for every stage of an organization's life — the founder in year one who has just been asked for financials for the first time, the established company that needs the monthly story told in board language, and the nonprofit whose board meets in six weeks. A fraction of the cost; never a fraction of the quality.

The team

The people you will speak with

Your first conversation is with a certified account executive who carries a region and a book, and who is trained on the product before they are trusted with a conversation. They will ask about your board, your calendar and what your bookkeeper hands you today — and they will tell you plainly whether we are the right fit.

One reviewer

Every packet, one signature

However you reach us, the work is prepared under one process and reviewed by one person. Nothing is delivered until the founder has read it line by line and signed it. That is the standard, and it does not change with the size of the engagement.
