Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026. This policy covers the Engerman Solutions website and the forms on it. Client files and engagement data are governed by the Service Terms and Engagement Schedule each client signs; where this policy and a signed agreement differ, the agreement controls.

Who we are

E-Solutions Consulting, LLC, doing business as Engerman Solutions ("we", "us"), Pennsylvania, USA. Contact: info@engermansolutions.com.

What we collect on this site

  • What you give us through a form: your name, role, organization, email, and the answers you choose to provide (for example your board's meeting cadence or accounting system). We use it to respond to you and, if you become a client, to set up your engagement.
  • Email you send us: the contents and your address, used to reply.
  • Website analytics: we use a privacy-respecting analytics beacon from our hosting provider to count visits and page views. It does not use cookies to identify you across sites.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use client data to train models.
  • We do not ask for bank statements, personal identifiers of your staff, or anything a form on this site does not need.

Client files

Ledger exports, budgets and contracts are never submitted through website forms. They are exchanged through a secure client workspace set up at onboarding. Raw files are deleted thirty days after each packet is delivered; delivered packets remain in the client's workspace for the life of the engagement, with a full export on request at its end.

Retention

Form submissions and email are retained for as long as needed to respond and, for clients, for the life of the engagement plus the period required by law. You may ask us to delete your form submission at any time.

Service providers

We use reputable providers for website hosting, email, form handling and file storage. They process data on our instructions and are bound by their own privacy commitments. We do not permit them to use your information for their own purposes.

Your choices

Write to info@engermansolutions.com to see, correct or delete the information you have given us, or to unsubscribe from The Board-Ready Brief (every issue also carries an unsubscribe link).

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it.
