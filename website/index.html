What you receive

Three layers, one packet

You are buying the three-page Board Brief. The other two layers exist so your treasurer, your auditor and your lender can defend it.

Layer 1 · what the board uses

The Board Brief

Three pages. Where you stand against plan and last year, the handful of things to know, and the decisions for the meeting with the wording drafted — readable in five minutes, and it says plainly when nothing is being asked.

Layer 2 · the evidence

The Management Summary

One short section per subject — variance with evidence, forecast and cash, financial position, risks, decisions, adjustments and how the packet was checked — so any question from the table can be answered from it.

Layer 3 · the engine

The Budget Framework

Your budget spread month by month with last year beside it, full-year forecast, cash and reserves, scenarios and built-in checks — built at onboarding, kept current, yours. The memo summarizes; the workbook holds the detail.
